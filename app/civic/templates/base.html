Demo controls

Break the services on purpose and watch what happens to the letter. Real means the Public AI API, behind a local switch. Simulated services only show where a request went.

"Received" counts what actually reached each service. Services a rule excludes must stay at zero for letters under that rule. Social services: Switzerland only. School: Switzerland, then the EU. Other offices: Switzerland and the EU; the United States only if the resident agrees.
