 👉 Pre-check-in
 Pick up your badge early on Tuesday, Sep 22 or Wednesday, Sep 23 from 16:00 to 19:00 at Kraftwerk in Container K4 (Team Room) . 
 🔑 Must-haves 
 Valid photo ID or passport (required for check-in)
 Ticket QR code (sent to your email)
 Laptop & charger — outlets in Switzerland are Type J (don't forget an adapter if you need one!) 
 🔌 Essentials 
 Refillable water bottle / cup (water refill stations available throughout the venue)
 Change of clothes & toiletries (the venue is open 24/7 with bathrooms and a relaxation area, but no beds — see 🧘 Relax & Recharge
)
 Swimwear & towel (complimentary access to the indoor pool across the street — see 🧘 Relax & Recharge
) 
 🎧 Smart extras 
 Headphones (crucial for deep focus and late-night debugging playlists)
 Power bank & extra cables (for your phone and accessories)
 Notebook & pen (sometimes sketching ideas is faster than typing)
 Your favorite personal snacks (meals and drinks are fully provided, but it never hurts to have your go-to coding fuel)
